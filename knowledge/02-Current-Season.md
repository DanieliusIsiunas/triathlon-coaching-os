## Development priorities

1. **Running skill and durability**
   - Build distinct easy, controlled-fast and race-specific running gears.
   - Improve speed reserve without rapidly increasing running intensity and volume together.
   - Progress long-run durability gradually toward the marathon while monitoring calf, Achilles, knee and general recovery.
   - Treat running as the largest performance opportunity for Project 2:49.
2. **Bike-to-run transfer**
   - Test sustainable cycling cadence instead of treating the hardest gear as the performance target.
   - Preserve regular short transition runs when the training phase allows.
3. **Swimming**
   - Maintain frequency and efficiency.
   - Improve open-water sighting, straight-line execution and technical quality under fatigue.
4. **Fueling**
   - Preserve the successfully tolerated frequent-intake pattern.
   - Extend carbohydrate, fluid and sodium practice from Olympic-distance racing to marathon and later long-course requirements.
5. **Consistency**
   - Use a rolling session queue compatible with family life.
   - Protect recovery and avoid making up missed sessions by stacking load.
